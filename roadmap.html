MUSE HOSTING / ROADMAP

From nice idea to testable system.

A practical sequence for turning the hosting concept into a decision-ready prototype—without skipping the boring reliability work.

Phase 1 · Validate the premise

Re-measure storage; verify provider limits and policies; define what “host on Muse” technically means; confirm public access, uptime, and data durability constraints.

Phase 2 · Specify the smallest service

Choose one static site and a tiny set of files. Define upload, listing, download, permissions, quotas, logs, backup, and delete behavior. Keep a machine-readable manifest.

Phase 3 · Prototype safely

Use a non-production domain and non-sensitive test content. Add authentication, least privilege, integrity checks, monitoring, backup and restore drills, and a custom error route.

Phase 4 · Review and decide

Compare cost, time, maintainability, compliance, security, availability, rollback, and exit effort against the existing provider setup. Keep a documented decision record. Migration is optional; the goal is informed choice.

Every capability in this bundle is a concept or documentation artifact. No backend or hosting connector is active.
